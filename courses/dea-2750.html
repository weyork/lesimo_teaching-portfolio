Course

Overview

DEA 2750 explores how light shapes the experience and function of interior environments. Two studio projects connect hands-on experimentation with professional lighting documentation. In Luminaire Design, students test how materials and construction affect the quality of light, develop a sketch model, and refine it into a full-scale luminaire. They communicate their design intent and assembly through a concept statement, branding, a dimensioned section, and an exploded isometric drawing.

TA roles

  • Provided feedback during sketch-model reviews, helping students refine material choices, light effects, and assembly before building full-scale luminaires.
  • Reviewed sectional and exploded isometric drawings with students to clarify dimensions, material specifications, and how components fit together.
  • Guided students in translating lighting concepts into reflected ceiling plans and coordinating fixture layouts with ceiling conditions and maintenance access.
  • Helped students check fixture selections against manufacturer cut sheets, compile fixture schedules, and calculate lighting power density from total wattage and floor area.
  • Assisted with gallery pin-up and model installation for final luminaire critiques, where students presented full-scale prototypes alongside their technical drawings.

Culminating project

Lighting Space : Architectural Lighting

In Architectural Lighting, students develop a lighting scheme for a previously designed interior or propose a relighting scheme for an existing campus space. They consider how lighting supports activities and shapes the experience of the space, then document their decisions through a scaled reflected ceiling plan, a fixture schedule, manufacturer cut sheets, and a lighting power density calculation.
