DEA 6100 · Spring 2024

Studies in Design Thinking

Graduate design-thinking studio — teams frame problems and deliver report, presentation, and video.

teaching assistant design methodology focus group old adults with MCI

Course

Overview

This interdisciplinary course combines lectures, hands-on workshops, and two industry-led design challenges to teach human-centered problem-solving. In a semester-long team project, students move from user needs assessment and problem framing to prototyping and evaluation planning as they design technologies that support independent living and community participation for older adults with cognitive impairment.

Instructor

Role

TA roles

  • Participant Outreach & Focus Group Coordination — Reached out to prospective participants and organized focus groups so student teams could collect firsthand feedback from their intended users.
  • Qualitative Analysis Support — Helped teams analyze focus group data, identify recurring themes, and translate findings into design priorities.
