Course

Overview

This intermediate-level studio combines spatial user experience and service design to explore how brand identity, interiors, and custom furnishings shape the way people use and feel about a service. In Fall 2024, students worked with nonprofit clients on two community-engaged projects, using immersive VR and metaverse platforms to test and communicate their proposals through realistic use scenarios.

Design studio

TA roles

  • Research Guidance — Guided Bike Walk Tompkins teams in using observations, interviews, and surveys to identify user needs. Helped students translate their findings and the client’s goals into design priorities that connected brand strategy with spatial or product design.
  • Design Guidance — Gave feedback during informal pin-ups and project reviews, helping teams compare alternatives and refine their proposals based on user needs and client goals.
  • Studio Communication & Student Support — Helped teams prepare final presentations that clearly explained their design decisions and the evidence behind them. Answered students’ questions about design tools, coursework, and navigating studio and academic life.

Studio project

Project 1 — Bike Walk Tompkins

In Project 1, students form teams around shared interests and pair brand strategy with either spatial or product design. They use observations, surveys, and interviews to understand user needs, then develop and test their concepts through 3D prototyping and advanced visualization. The resulting proposals connect the client’s brand and goals with how people use and experience the service.

Studio project

Project 2 — Nimbus Floating Hotel

In Project 2, students design an airborne hotel experience, tracing the guest journey from ascent through flight and landing. They use immersive VR to explore how each phase shapes guests’ perception of space, comfort, sense of safety, and feeling of adventure. Working with the client, students consider structural requirements and material choices for safety and sustainability as they develop proposals for hotel interiors, furnishings, and products.
