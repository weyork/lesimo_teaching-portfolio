DEA 1140 · Spring 2026

Principles of  Design Computing

Students build foundational skills in digital design communication through 2D graphics, technical drawing, and 3D modeling and rendering, then explore generative AI as part of a collaborative design project.

design computing digital tools 3d rendering

Course

Overview

This course introduces the principles and digital tools that underpin design communication in Design and Environmental Analysis. Students begin with 2D graphics and technical drawing before moving on to 3D modeling and rendering. In the final module, they explore generative AI as part of a collaborative project to design, curate, and present an exhibition proposal. Through lectures, hands-on exercises, and projects, students build confidence in selecting and applying digital tools to communicate design ideas.

Individual project

Project 1: Mood Board

Students create a professional-quality mood board in Adobe Photoshop to communicate an interior design concept. They use composition, layering, and raster image editing to create visual depth and express a clear, cohesive design intent.

Group project

Final Project: Fashion Rebrand & Pop-up Store Design

Working in pairs, students reimagine the identity of an existing fashion brand in the United States and translate that identity into a retail pop-up at the Jill Stuart Gallery in Cornell’s Human Ecology Building. The project brings together the technical skills, design thinking methods, and creative practices students develop throughout the semester.
