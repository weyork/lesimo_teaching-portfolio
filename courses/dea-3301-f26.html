DEA 3301 · Fall 2026

Design UX with Technology Studio

Intermediate studio — UX methods for commercial, learning, and workplace interiors, with digital tools to simulate how people experience designed space.

teaching assistant user experience wayfinding studio critique

Course

Overview

This intermediate-level studio teaches students to design and evaluate how people experience spaces and services. Students combine human-centered design and evidence-based research with immersive VR, digital human evaluation, and A/B testing to test and refine their proposals. In Fall 2026, students apply these methods to two projects, one with Cornell’s campus community and one with an external client, to develop and communicate design solutions for real stakeholders.

Design studio

TA roles

  • Research Guidance — Helped student teams use site observations and user research to identify a spatial experience problem in Martha Van Rensselaer Hall or the Human Ecology Building. Guided teams in planning A/B user tests to compare design alternatives against a shared baseline.
  • Design Guidance — Gave feedback during studio critiques to help teams translate research findings into decisions about layout, ergonomics, lighting, and materials. Helped teams working on CannonDesign’s Buffalo office connect their proposals to the firm’s workplace needs and use client feedback to refine their designs.
  • Studio Communication & Student Support — Helped teams communicate the evidence behind their design decisions in project reports, visual materials, and presentations. Answered questions about assignments, design tools, and navigating studio and academic life.

Studio project

Mid-review with CannonDesign

Project 2 — students partner with CannonDesign to develop proposals for an undesigned space and adjacent areas in CannonDesign’s Buffalo office. Working in the same teams, students research workplace needs, prototype spatial and experiential improvements, and test their proposals through user studies. The project culminates in a professional pitch and final deliverables presented to the firm.
